## 1. Design Direction

The frontend visual system was refined from a functional prototype into a recruiter-facing AI SaaS product design system (inspired by OpenAI, Linear, and Vercel dark interfaces):

- **Background & Lighting**: Deep Navy base (`#0B0F19`) with radial ambient glow blooms (`bg-glow-radial`) and fine noise background grid overlays (`bg-grid-pattern`).
- **Glassmorphism**: Translucent card surfaces (`rgba(19, 26, 42, 0.72)`), 16px backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`), modest borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and subtle hover elevation (`glass-panel-hover`).
- **Color Palette**: Violet (`#8B5CF6`), Magenta (`#EC4899`), Cyan (`#06B6D4`), Emerald (`#10B981`), Amber (`#F59E0B`).
- **Typography**: High-impact **Inter** typeface hierarchy with custom gradient headings and clear font-weight distinctions.
