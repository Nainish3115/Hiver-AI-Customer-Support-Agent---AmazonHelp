# AmazonHelp Customer Intent Human Annotation Guidelines

> **Target Brand**: `AmazonHelp`  
> **Dataset File**: [`data/golden/amazonhelp_gold_200_annotation.csv`](file:///D:/hiver-support-agent/data/golden/amazonhelp_gold_200_annotation.csv) (200 cases)  
> **Annotation Purpose**: Assign single-label, human-verified gold intent annotations across the approved 17-intent taxonomy for benchmark evaluation.

---

## 1. Overview & General Annotation Rules

1. **Single Primary Intent Assignment**: Every customer query must be assigned **exactly one** final intent label from the 17 approved categories.
2. **Classify Customer Request Only**: Base your annotation strictly on the customer's initial problem text (`customer_text`). Do **not** infer intent from how support agents responded.
3. **Primary Action Rule (Multi-Intent Queries)**: If a customer query mentions multiple issues (e.g. complaining about a late delivery and asking for a refund), annotate the **primary actionable request** (e.g. `refund_status_request` if refund is demanded, or `late_delivery_shipping_delay` if delivery tracking is demanded).
4. **Fallback Rule (`general_inquiry_other`)**: Assign `general_inquiry_other` **only** if the inquiry cannot be categorized into any of the 16 specific intent categories after applying tie-breaking rules.
5. **Reference Field**: The `suggested_intent` column is provided as a non-binding reference generated by automated heuristics. You must verify and override it if incorrect.

## 2. Intent Taxonomy Definitions, Criteria & Examples

### 1. `order_tracking_delivery_status`
- **Definition**: Customer inquiring about current shipment location, carrier tracking updates, or estimated time of arrival (ETA) before or on the delivery date.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: Mention of tracking numbers, carrier status, "where is my order", "when will it ship/arrive".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Order is confirmed overdue or delayed past estimated date (use `late_delivery_shipping_delay`).
- **Example**: *"Where is my package? The tracking info hasn't updated since yesterday."*

### 2. `late_delivery_shipping_delay`
- **Definition**: Package delivery is past its promised delivery date/time window, or explicitly flagged as delayed by the carrier.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: Explicit mention of missed delivery date, delayed package, late arrival.
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Package delivered to wrong address (use `address_delivery_instructions` or `missing_package_delivered_not_received`).
- **Example**: *"My Prime 2-day delivery was supposed to arrive yesterday at 5pm. Still not here!"*

### 3. `missing_package_delivered_not_received`
- **Definition**: Tracking status shows "Delivered" or "Handed to resident", but customer claims they did not receive the package.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Says delivered but I don't have it", "Stolen package", "Checked porch/front door, nothing there".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Address was incorrect on order (use `address_delivery_instructions`).
- **Example**: *"Amazon tracking says delivered to front porch at 2pm, but I was home and no package arrived!"*

### 4. `refund_status_request`
- **Definition**: Inquiries regarding refund progress, reimbursement processing timelines, or status of credited funds.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "When will I get my refund", "Refund not showing in bank account", "Item returned, where is money".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Requesting instructions on how to initiate a return (use `return_exchange_inquiry`).
- **Example**: *"Returned the item 5 days ago and dropped it off at UPS. When will my refund be processed?"*

### 5. `return_exchange_inquiry`
- **Definition**: Questions regarding return policies, return window, creating return shipping labels, or exchanging an item.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "How do I return this", "Need a return label", "Can I exchange for a different size".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Customer is asking about refund money status for an already returned item (use `refund_status_request`).
- **Example**: *"How can I get a printable return shipping label for a shirt that is too small?"*

### 6. `order_cancellation_request`
- **Definition**: Requesting to cancel an order, stop dispatch, or cancel an scheduled delivery service.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Please cancel order #123", "Cancel my assembly service", "Accidentally ordered, need to cancel".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Cancelling a recurring Prime subscription (use `prime_membership_subscription`).
- **Example**: *"I accidentally placed order 104-9283742-123456. Please cancel it immediately before it ships!"*

### 7. `damaged_defective_item`
- **Definition**: Product arrived broken, torn, physically damaged, non-functional, or defective upon arrival.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Broken box/screen", "Item doesn't turn on", "Damaged in transit", "Defective product".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Seller sent a completely different product (use `wrong_item_received`).
- **Example**: *"Opened my package and the glass jar inside was completely shattered."*

### 8. `wrong_item_received`
- **Definition**: Received a product different from what was ordered (wrong model, wrong size, missing included parts/accessories).
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Ordered X but got Y", "Wrong item in box", "Received 1 item instead of 2-pack".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Product arrived broken/defective (use `damaged_defective_item`).
- **Example**: *"I ordered a blue XL t-shirt, but received a pair of black socks instead!"*

### 9. `payment_billing_issue`
- **Definition**: Double charges, payment authorization failures, gift card balance errors, promo code billing errors, or invoice queries.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Charged twice for order", "Payment declined", "Gift card code not applying", "Unauthorized charge".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Prime subscription fee issues (use `prime_membership_subscription`).
- **Example**: *"My credit card was charged twice for order 112-3849201-998877."*

### 10. `account_access_login`
- **Definition**: Inability to log in, password reset issues, OTP/2FA verification code errors, or account suspension/lockout.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Can't log into my account", "Not receiving OTP code", "Account locked", "Password reset link broken".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Inquiries about Prime membership benefits (use `prime_membership_subscription`).
- **Example**: *"I'm trying to reset my password but I'm not receiving the verification code email."*

### 11. `prime_membership_subscription`
- **Definition**: Questions or complaints regarding Amazon Prime membership, auto-renewal fees, Prime Student, or cancellation of Prime.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Charged $14.99 Prime fee", "Cancel Prime trial", "Prime delivery benefits not working".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Cancelling a specific product order (use `order_cancellation_request`).
- **Example**: *"I was charged for Prime renewal today but I wanted to cancel my trial!"*

### 12. `digital_content_kindle_video`
- **Definition**: Streaming issues on Prime Video, Kindle e-book sync errors, Audible downloads, or digital music licenses.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Kindle book won't download", "Prime Video error code", "Audible credit missing".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Physical book arrived damaged (use `damaged_defective_item`).
- **Example**: *"Prime Video gives me error code 5004 whenever I try to play a movie."*

### 13. `third_party_seller_issue`
- **Definition**: Disputes, lack of response, or haranguing messages from third-party marketplace sellers on Amazon.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Seller won't reply", "Third-party seller spamming review requests", "Marketplace seller refused refund".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Item sold directly by Amazon (use standard return/refund/delivery intents).
- **Example**: *"Bought an item from third-party seller @115821 and they keep sending spam emails demanding 5-star reviews."*

### 14. `promotions_discounts_coupons`
- **Definition**: Lightning deals, discount coupon codes, price match requests, or promotional credit applications.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Coupon code not working", "Price dropped after purchase", "Promotional $10 credit missing".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Payment billing errors (use `payment_billing_issue`).
- **Example**: *"Applied promo code HOLIDAY10 at checkout but the $10 discount was not deducted."*

### 15. `address_delivery_instructions`
- **Definition**: Changing delivery address, updating gate codes, driver delivery instructions, or wrong delivery location.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Change shipping address for order", "Leave at back door", "Driver left package at wrong neighbor".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Tracking inquiries on undelivered packages (use `order_tracking_delivery_status`).
- **Example**: *"Need to update the delivery instructions for my order to leave it with the building doorman."*

### 16. `customer_service_complaint`
- **Definition**: Severe customer dissatisfaction with support agent behavior, phone call hang-ups, unhelpful chat agents, or poor service quality.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: "Agent hung up on me", "Customer service was rude", "Rude support experience", "Going in circles with support".
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Customer complaining about a late package without mentioning support agent behavior (use `late_delivery_shipping_delay`).
- **Example**: *"Customer service rep hung up on me after keeping me on hold for 45 minutes!"*

### 17. `general_inquiry_other`
- **Definition**: Fallback category for general inquiries, greetings, or obscure requests not covered above.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: Unspecific greetings ("Hello Amazon"), general feedback, ambiguous queries lacking transaction context.
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Any query that fits one of the 16 specific intent classes above.
- **Example**: *"Hello, how can I talk to someone?"*

## 3. Decision Boundary & Tie-Breaking Rules

| Category Pair | Ambiguity / Conflict | Tie-Breaking Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `order_tracking_delivery_status` vs `late_delivery_shipping_delay` | Customer asks for tracking info because item hasn't arrived. | If the delivery date **has passed** or customer states it is **late/delayed**, annotate `late_delivery_shipping_delay`. If delivery date is in future or customer simply asks for **ETA/location**, annotate `order_tracking_delivery_status`. |
| `refund_status_request` vs `return_exchange_inquiry` | Customer mentions returning an item and getting money back. | If customer is asking **how to return / needs a label**, annotate `return_exchange_inquiry`. If customer has **already returned / shipped** and is asking where the money is, annotate `refund_status_request`. |
| `damaged_defective_item` vs `wrong_item_received` | Customer received an item that is different or broken. | If the item is what was ordered but is **broken/functional flaw**, annotate `damaged_defective_item`. If the item is **completely different product/size**, annotate `wrong_item_received`. |
| `customer_service_complaint` vs Transactional Intents | Customer expresses anger while describing a transactional issue. | If the anger is directed at **support agent conduct / call dropped / rude service**, annotate `customer_service_complaint`. If anger is directed at **the order issue itself**, annotate the underlying transactional intent. |
